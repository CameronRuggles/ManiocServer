# Naming addendum — the bird direction, checked properly
Prepared 2026-10-16 ~11:35 MDT (17:35 UTC), after Cameron floated a bird name ("since the server and such are nested"), the xkcd/nonsense idea, Rootcellr, and an external critique of Rootcellr that Muse refereed in chat.
No name is decided. This addendum does for the bird genera what the decision sheet did for the wasps: story, domain status verified, costs stated plainly.

## Method note (why these checks can be trusted)
All .org checks below were run against the registry RDAP at `rdap.publicinterestregistry.org/rdap/domain/<name>` after validating the endpoint against controls (wikipedia.org and rootcellar.org both correctly return 200/registered). All .com checks via Verisign RDAP (google.com control = 200). 404 = unregistered.
Side benefit: the wasp/cellar .org names from the decision sheet were re-validated on the same corrected endpoint — **polistes.org, annularis.org, associus.org, rootcllr.org, rootcellr.org, rtcellar.org and getrootcellar.org all still 404 (unregistered)**. The sheet's domain claims stand.

## The three that matter

### Seiurus — the cleanest domain sweep since Rootcellr, with none of its taxes
- The ovenbird *Seiurus aurocapilla* is the genus's only species, and its nest is the architecture diagram: a covered dome on the forest floor with a single side entrance. Sealed box, one door — the Tor gateway story.
- Both .com and .org are free in the **natural spelling**. That escapes both taxes the Rootcellr critique named: no vowel-drop/2008-startup tone, and no hear-it-type-it leak to a taken natural spelling, because this IS the natural spelling.
- Costs, honestly: it says nothing about birds, nests or servers until explained (same "inert until explained, then excellent" profile as Polistes), and the mouth test is mid-hard — roughly "see-YUR-us," three syllables, spellable once heard a couple of times. Easier than Polistes/Annularis, harder than Rootcellar.

### Anthoscopus — the best security story, the worst mouth test
- The penduline tits of this genus build the most security-minded nest in birds: a conspicuous entrance opens into an empty false chamber, while the real chamber hides behind a flap the parent opens, enters, and seals shut with sticky spider silk. Decoy door, hidden real door, sealed behind you — a striking match for a hidden-service box.
- Both .com and .org free, natural spelling.
- Costs: 11 letters, the hardest pronunciation/spelling of any live candidate ("an-tho-SCOPE-us"), and the Greek parses as "flower-searcher," which is off-message. Story is superb in a launch post and a burden in every spoken mention.

### Philetairus — the best containers story, with a domain asterisk
- The sociable weaver *Philetairus socius* builds a single massive communal structure — one common thatched roof (maintained by all), under which sit up to 250+ separate chambers that **do not interconnect**, each reached by its own entrance tunnel. One roof = the OS; many isolated chambers = the sites/containers; no chamber opens into another. Arguably the most exact nesting/containers analogy of any candidate in any direction, wasps included.
- Bonus resonance: its species epithet is *socius* — "companion/ally," the same root Cameron liked in Associus.
- Costs: .org is free but **.com could not be confirmed** (Verisign and rdap.org both timed out on repeated tries at ~17:30 UTC — re-check before relying on it). Name is long and unfamiliar; "weaver" warmth, zero instant meaning.

## Where this leaves the field
- The bird direction is NOT killed by the domain tax the way the plain words suggested in chat — the genus route works for birds exactly as it did for wasps, and Seiurus matches the wasp pair's domain position (Polistes/Annularis: .org free, .com taken or other-owned) and beats it on .com.
- Rootcellr after the external critique: mechanically fine domains (both still free, re-validated today), but it pays the startup-tone tax and the family hear-it-type-it leak; Muse downgraded it in chat and this addendum doesn't rehabilitate it.
- Nothing registered; registration remains Cameron's explicit call. If a bird name goes to the two external AIs, it needs its own unbiased prompt in the same neutral style as the Polistes+Annularis and Rootcllr prompts in the decision sheet — none exists yet for any bird candidate.
