=====================================================================
ENVIRONMENT INCIDENT (affects the apt-installed tools above)
=====================================================================
The audit VM was REPLACED mid-session (observed 2026-10-18 ~18:34
UTC: uptime reset, all apt-installed packages gone; everything under
~ persisted, including ~/audit-tools and this workspace). The host
runtime's package-intent replay then reinstalled the recorded apt
packages automatically; while it ran (~25 min), the dpkg frontend
lock was held and fresh installs failed. During that window,
unsquashfs/xorriso were ALSO obtained by `apt-get download` +
`dpkg -x` into ~/audit-tools/root/ (Ubuntu noble .debs, run with
LD_LIBRARY_PATH) — the Phase 2 extraction used those copies;
versions identical to the apt set. Lintian 2.117 via download was
impractical (74 dependencies) and was run after the replay restored
the apt install.
